Privacy notice

University Command Center is a free study tool run by an individual student developer (the "site owner"), not by a university or company. This notice explains what we keep about you, why, who else handles it, and your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Contact: {{ contact }}. Email this address for any privacy question or request.

What we keep

  • Your account: username, email address, when you confirmed your email and when you last signed in. Your password is never stored; only a one-way scrambled form of it (a hash) is kept.
  • What you add: semesters, courses, credit units, grades, assignments, classes in your timetable, study plans, your optional profile (display name, institution, programme, level, matric number, class bands), study files you upload (PDF or text) and the text read from them so you can search them.
  • AI tools: a record of each AI request (which tool, when, how much text, and whether it worked), plus the results and questions you choose to keep. The record does not keep your file's text.
  • Security records: counters that slow down password guessing and repeated sign-ups. They store scrambled values (keyed hashes) of your username or network address, not the plain values, and they expire. Sign-up codes are stored scrambled and deleted a day after they expire.

We do not show adverts, sell data, or use tracking or analytics cookies.

Cookies

  • Sign-in session (__Host-ucc_session): keeps you signed in. It ends when you close your browser, and the server ends it after 30 minutes without activity.
  • Form protection (__Host-ucc_csrf): stops other websites from submitting forms as you.
  • Sign-in security: may remember this browser for up to 30 days so suspicious sign-ins can be slowed down.
  • Preferences (ucc_display, ucc_ai_notes): your light/dark choice and whether to shorten the AI notes. Kept for a year.

Why we use it

  • To run the service you signed up for: your account, records, calculations, files and search.
  • To keep it secure: confirming email addresses, limiting abuse and protecting other students' data.
  • To send AI requests, only when you press Send for a file.

Who else handles it

  • Vercel runs the website on servers in Dublin, Ireland. Its firewall and logs briefly record technical details such as your network (IP) address, the page and the time, for security and to keep the site running.
  • Supabase stores the database, including your study files, in Ireland (EU). Each student's records are locked so other students cannot read them.
  • GitHub (owned by Microsoft) holds our daily backups. Each backup is encrypted before it leaves the database, and only the site owner holds the key that can open it.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers our emails: your email address and the sign-up code or reset link.
  • Google Gemini receives, only when you press Send, the text of the file you chose (up to 60,000 characters) and your question if you asked one. We do not send your name, username, email address, matric number, file name or title. We use Gemini's free service, so Google may keep what is sent, use it to improve its products, and people at Google may read it. Do not send anything you would not want shared, and never send other people's personal information.

These companies are outside Nigeria (in the European Union and the United States). We use them because they provide strong security and legal protections for the data they handle.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and records: until you delete them. You can delete your courses, files, AI results and other records yourself at any time, or delete your whole account in Settings → Delete account, which removes everything straight away.
  • Encrypted backups: a copy is made every day and each copy is deleted automatically after 30 days. So something you delete, or a deleted account, can remain inside an encrypted backup for up to 30 days, and is only used if the site has to be restored after a failure.
  • Sign-in sessions, sign-up codes and security counters: minutes to days, then they are removed automatically.
  • Information already sent to Google Gemini follows Google's own rules; deleting a result here does not delete it at Google.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to:

  • see the information we hold about you, and get a copy. In your workspace, Settings → Export my data downloads your records at any time;
  • correct anything wrong, which you can also do yourself in the app;
  • delete your information, or your whole account yourself in Settings → Delete account;
  • object to or limit how we use it, and withdraw consent to AI use simply by not pressing Send.

Email {{ contact }} from the address on your account. We aim to reply within 30 days. If you are not happy with our answer you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

Security

Connections use HTTPS. The website signs in to the database with restricted logins, and the database itself checks that every academic record belongs to the signed-in student. Passwords are hashed and sign-in attempts are limited. No system is perfectly secure: if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

Age

This site is for university students. If you are under 18, use it only with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes too, and important changes will be shown on the site or emailed to you.
