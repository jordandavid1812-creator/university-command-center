How to read your results

A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, E=1 and F=0. F contributes attempted credits; an ungraded course contributes neither points nor attempted credits. Overall CGPA uses all attempted credits, rather than averaging semester GPAs.

Display order is for organization and does not imply semester chronology.
