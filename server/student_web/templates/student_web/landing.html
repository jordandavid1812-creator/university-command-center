What you can do

Know your GPA and CGPA

  • GPA for each semester and your overall CGPA, on the 5-point or 4-point scale.
  • See how every number is worked out, step by step.
  • Where you stand for First Class, 2:1, 2:2 and the rest.
  • A predictor and a target planner for the grades you need. They never change your saved grades.

Stay organised

  • Your courses and semesters, in your own order.
  • Assignments with due dates, and what is due this week.
  • A weekly class timetable.
  • Study plans that spread your topics out before each exam.

Study help from your notes

  • Upload your notes and search all of them at once.
  • Turn a file into a summary, a quiz or flashcards, or ask a question about it.
  • Nothing is sent until you check and confirm it. A small daily allowance keeps it free.

Free and private

  • Free to use.
  • Only you can see your records.
  • Download all your data, or delete your account, at any time.

Ready in minutes

How it works

  1. Create an account. We email you a 6-digit code to confirm your address.
  2. Add your courses and grades. Enter the letter grade and credit units for each course.
  3. See where you stand. Your GPA, CGPA and plans update straight away.
