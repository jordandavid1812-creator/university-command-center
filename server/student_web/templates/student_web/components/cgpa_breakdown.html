How your CGPA is calculated

  1. Each grade has points: {{ scale.points|default:'A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, E=1, F=0' }}.
  2. For each course, quality points = grade points × credit units.
  3. Semester GPA = that semester's quality points ÷ its graded credit units.
  4. CGPA = all quality points ÷ all graded credit units (not an average of semester GPAs). Ungraded courses count towards nothing.
